Buying & reserving

Browse bikes
How does reserving online work?

Pay the £99 deposit on the bike's page. The bike comes off sale straight away and the £99 comes off the price. An account is made for you so you can follow the prep.

Is the £99 deposit refundable?

Yes, if the bike fails its pre-sale checks. The full reservation terms are shown before you pay.

What checks has every bike had?

An HPI check, our 120-point inspection, an MOT where needed and a full valet. You can see each stage tick off in your account.

Can I see the bike before I buy?

Yes. Visit the showroom, watch the walkaround video on the bike's page, or ask us for a live video call.

How do I pay the balance?

By bank transfer, card or finance, less any part-exchange. The balance is due before collection or delivery.

Do you sell 125cc and learner bikes?

Yes. Use the 125cc and learner filter on our motorbikes page to see what is in stock.

Finance

Finance options
What is the representative APR?

19.9% APR representative. For example, a £6,495 bike over 48 months with no deposit is £191.77 a month, £9,204.73 in total. Finance is subject to status and affordability.

Can I get finance with poor credit?

Often, yes. Apply online and we will look at the options available to you. Every application is subject to status.

How long can I spread the cost?

Usually 2 to 5 years. Use the calculator on the finance page to see monthly figures.

Do I need a deposit for finance?

Not always. You can put down cash or use your part-exchange as a deposit to lower the monthly payment.

Can I reserve first and apply later?

Yes. Reserve for £99, then apply from your account before your collection or delivery date.

Part-exchange & selling

Value my bike
How do I get a part-exchange valuation?

Enter your registration and mileage on the part-exchange page or on any bike page. We confirm the figure when we see the bike.

Can I part-exchange if I still owe finance?

Usually, yes. Tell us when you ask for a valuation and we will talk you through it.

Can I sell you my bike without buying one?

Yes. Use sell your bike for a valuation. We buy bikes outright.

What happens to my part-exchange on delivery day?

Tell us when you reserve and we will agree the handover of your old bike with you.

What do I need to bring?

All keys, the V5C logbook, the service history and any MOT certificates you have.

Delivery & collection

Delivery prices
Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in mainland UK. For islands, call us before you reserve.

How much is delivery?

It is priced per mile from our showroom by road. Enter your postcode on the delivery page or at checkout for an instant price.

When do I pay for delivery?

At checkout, with your £99 deposit. The price is fixed once paid.

Is collection free?

Yes. Collect from the showroom at a time we agree. We call you to book it in.

Do I need to be in?

Yes, or someone you trust. The handover checklist has to be signed and we hand over the keys and paperwork in person.

Can I switch between collection and delivery?

Yes, any time before your bike is ready. Just call us.

Tracking my bike

Track my bike
What can I see in my account?

Each prep stage as our team ticks it off: deposit received, valet, MOT, 120-point inspection, ready for delivery, out for delivery and delivered. Photos are added as we go.

How does live tracking work?

On delivery day our driver shares the GPS from their phone. You see the van on a map with an arrival time that updates as they drive.

When does the tracking link appear?

As soon as the driver sets off. It shows in your account and we text you the link.

I collected my bike. Can I still use my account?

Yes. Your documents, invoice and warranty stay in your account for good.

I cannot log in. What do I do?

Ask for a sign-in code by text on the account page, or call us and we will sort it.

Warranty & returns

Warranty details
What warranty comes with every bike?

Every bike comes with 30 days' warranty as standard.

What does the red warranty badge mean?

Bikes with the red badge include longer cover free: 3, 6, 12 or 24 months, as shown on the badge.

Can I extend my warranty?

Yes. You can add longer cover to any bike at checkout. See the warranty page for options.

What is the 14-day money back?

If the bike is not right for you, you have 14 days to tell us. Call us first and we will explain how the return works.

What is the 120-point inspection?

Our technicians check engine, brakes, suspension, wheels, electrics and frame, then road test the bike. See the full checklist.

How do I make a warranty claim?

Call or message us with your order number. Your warranty documents are saved in your account.

Workshop

Book a service
Do you work on bikes not bought from you?

Yes. We service, MOT and repair all makes, bought from us or not.

How do I book an MOT?

Use the booking form on the workshop page. Enter your reg, pick a slot and we text to confirm.

Will you do extra work without asking?

No. If we find something beyond what you booked, we call you with a price first.

Do you fit tyres?

Yes. Bring your own or we order the right pair for you.

Will you stamp my service book?

Yes, and you get a written note of the work done.

Accessories orders

Shop accessories
Do you post accessories?

Yes, anywhere in the UK. Or pick your order up from the showroom.

Can I try on a helmet first?

Yes. Visit the showroom and our team will help you find the right size and fit.

Can I return something?

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned. Call us first and quote your order number.

Where do I see my accessories order?

In your account, under My orders.
