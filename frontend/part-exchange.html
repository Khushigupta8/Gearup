Your valuation

What affects the value?

We check the same things every buyer would. Be honest up front and the figure we quote is the figure you get.

Mileage Big

Lower miles for the age means a higher value. Give us the reading off the clock.

Service history Big

Stamps, receipts and dealer invoices all add value. Bring everything you have.

Condition Big

Scuffs, dents, worn tyres and corrosion. Photos help us price it fairly.

Demand Medium

Popular models and A2-friendly bikes sell quickly, so they are worth more to us.

MOT Medium

A long MOT saves us prep work. A recent advisory list is useful too.

Keys and logbook Medium

Two keys and a V5C in your name. Missing items cost money to replace.

Modifications Varies

Quality parts can help. Loud pipes and cut tails can lower the value. Keep the originals.

Time of year Small

Bikes sell faster in spring. We buy all year round and price fairly in winter too.

All FAQs
Is the online valuation the final price?

It is a guide based on what you tell us. We confirm the figure when we see the bike. If it matches your description and photos, the figure stays the same.

Can I part-ex a bike with finance on it?

Yes. We get a settlement figure from your lender and pay it off as part of the deal. If you owe more than the bike is worth, the difference is added to what you pay.

Do you take bikes that need work?

Yes, including non-runners. Tell us what is wrong and we will price it fairly.

Can you collect my old bike?

Yes. If we are delivering your new bike, we can take your part-ex away on the same trip.

What if I just want cash?

We buy bikes outright too. See sell your bike.
