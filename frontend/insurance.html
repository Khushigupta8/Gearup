Good to know

Insurance FAQs

Do I need insurance before I collect my bike?

Yes. You must be insured to ride away or have the bike delivered to the road. Get a quote here and set the start date to your collection or delivery day.

Can I insure a 125cc on a CBT?

Yes. Most insurers cover learner riders on a valid CBT. Make sure the L plates are on until you pass your test.

Does the price depend on where I keep the bike?

It does. Postcode, security (garage, ground anchor, alarm) and your riding history all affect the price.
