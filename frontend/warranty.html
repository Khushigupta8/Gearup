The cover

What's covered

Our warranty covers mechanical and electrical failure. It does not cover parts that wear out with normal riding. Full terms come with your bike.

Covered

  • Engine internal parts
  • Gearbox and selector
  • Clutch mechanism
  • Final drive and shaft
  • Fuel system and pump
  • Cooling system and water pump
  • Starter motor
  • Alternator and regulator
  • ECU and wiring
  • ABS pump and sensors
  • Instruments and switches
  • Parts and labour

Not covered

  • Wear items: tyres, brake pads, discs, chain and sprockets, clutch plates
  • Routine servicing, bulbs, fuses and batteries
  • Accident damage, theft or vandalism
  • Damage from neglect, missed services or running low on oil
  • Faults caused by modifications after you buy
  • Paint, plastics and cosmetic marks
  • Track days, racing or courier use
This is a summary. Your warranty booklet sets out the full terms, claim limits and what you need to do to keep cover valid. Your legal rights are not affected.

Making a claim

Something wrong? Here's what to do

Stop riding if it is not safe, then get in touch. Do not book repairs elsewhere before speaking to us, or the claim may not be covered.

Contact the workshop

  1. Step 01Tell us

    Call, email or WhatsApp with your order number and what the bike is doing.

  2. Step 02We diagnose

    Bring it to our workshop, or we arrange an approved garage near you.

  3. Step 03We confirm cover

    We tell you what is covered before any work starts. No surprises.

  4. Step 04We fix it

    Covered parts and labour are paid by the warranty. You get back on the road.

Keep your paperwork

Hold on to your service receipts. You may be asked for them when you claim.

Report it early

Tell us as soon as you notice a fault. Riding on can turn a small fix into a big one.

14-day money back

Changed your mind? Return the bike within 14 days as set out in our terms.

Warranty FAQs

Cover, explained

Questions about a particular bike? Ask us before you reserve.

All FAQs
When does my warranty start?

On the day you collect the bike or we deliver it to you.

Can I extend the warranty?

Yes, on most bikes, to 12 or 24 months. The price depends on the bike. Ask us when you reserve.

Does the warranty cover delivered bikes?

Yes. The cover is the same whether you collect or we deliver anywhere in mainland UK.

Do I have to service the bike with you?

No, but it must be serviced on time by a qualified garage, and you need to keep the receipts. Our workshop can do it for you.

Can I see the inspection report?

Yes. Every bike's 120-point report is on its bike page and in your account once you reserve.
