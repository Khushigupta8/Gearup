How it works

HP or PCP? Plain English.

Two ways to pay monthly. Both are fixed-rate loans secured on the bike. The difference is what happens at the end.

Finance questions
Most popular

Hire purchase (HP)

Pay a deposit, then fixed monthly payments. After the last payment the bike is yours. Simple.

  • Fixed payments that never change
  • No mileage limit
  • You own the bike at the end
  • Your part-exchange can be the deposit

Best for: riders who want to keep the bike and pay it off in full.

Ask about it

Personal contract purchase (PCP)

Lower monthly payments, with a larger final payment at the end. Then choose: pay it and keep the bike, hand it back, or trade it in.

  • Lower monthly cost than HP
  • Three choices at the end
  • Agreed annual mileage applies
  • Not available on every bike

Best for: riders who like to change bikes every few years. Ask us if PCP is available on the bike you want.

All FAQs
Will checking my eligibility affect my credit score?

Checking your eligibility uses a soft search where the lender offers one. Soft searches are not visible to other lenders. A full credit check only happens if you choose to go ahead with an application, and we will tell you before it does.

Can I get finance with bad credit?

Often, yes. Apply and we will look at the options available to you. Finance is always subject to status and affordability.

Do I need a deposit?

Not always. Many riders pay £0 deposit. A deposit or part-exchange lowers your monthly payment and the total interest you pay.

Can I use my old bike as the deposit?

Yes. Get a part-exchange valuation and the agreed value goes towards your deposit. If there is finance outstanding on your old bike, tell us and we can settle it as part of the deal.

Can I pay it off early?

Yes. You can settle a finance agreement early at any time. The lender will give you a settlement figure, which usually includes a reduction in interest.

Can I reserve the bike while finance is checked?

Yes. Pay the £99 reservation deposit and the bike comes off sale. The £99 comes off the price of the bike.
