Selling FAQs

Before you sell

Questions about your bike? WhatsApp us a few photos.

Contact us
How do I get paid?

By bank transfer once we have agreed the price and checked the bike and paperwork. If there is finance owing, we pay the lender first and send you the rest.

Will the price change when you see the bike?

Only if the bike is not as described. Be honest about marks and faults, add photos, and the figure we quote is the figure you get.

What paperwork do I need?

The V5C logbook in your name, photo ID, all keys, and any service history you have. We take care of telling the DVLA.

Can you collect the bike?

Yes, from anywhere in mainland UK. We will agree a collection time with you when we agree the price.

Do you buy bikes with outstanding finance?

Yes. Get a settlement figure from your lender. We pay it off and send you any balance.

Would I get more by part-exchanging?

If you are buying from us too, part-exchange can be simpler, and your bike becomes the deposit on your next one.
