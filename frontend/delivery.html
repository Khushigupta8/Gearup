Delivery day

What happens on the day

Your bike is prepped before it leaves. The day itself is simple.

  1. The day beforeTime window

    We text you a delivery window and the driver's name. Reply if it does not suit.

  2. Setting offTracking goes live

    The bike is strapped into our van. Your tracking link switches on as the driver leaves.

  3. At your doorHandover

    The driver unloads the bike and goes through the checklist below with you.

  4. DoneSign and ride

    Sign the handover sheet. Your documents and warranty are saved in your account.

Handover

The handover checklist

The same checklist at the showroom or at your door. Nothing is signed until you are happy.

We go through with you
  • Walk-round of the bike against the advert photos
  • Mileage and condition noted on the sheet
  • Keys handed over
  • Controls, dash and any extras explained
  • Service book and history
  • MOT certificate and HPI report
  • 120-point inspection sheet
  • Warranty documents
  • V5C new keeper details
  • Handover sheet signed by both of us

Have these ready

  • Photo ID, such as your driving licence
  • Balance paid before the day, or finance signed
  • Insurance in place from the delivery date
  • Space on flat ground to unload
  • Your part-exchange bike, keys and V5C, if you have one

Delivery questions

The ones we get asked most. More answers on our help page.

All delivery FAQs
Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in mainland UK. For islands and anywhere off the mainland, call us before you reserve.

How is the delivery price worked out?

We take the road miles from our showroom to your postcode and multiply by our rate per mile, with a minimum charge. Enter your postcode above to see your price.

When do I pay for delivery?

At checkout, with your £99 reservation deposit. The price is fixed once you have paid.

How does the live tracking work?

Our driver shares their phone's GPS while they drive. On delivery day a link appears in your account and by text, showing the van on a map and an updated arrival time.

What if I am not happy at the handover?

Tell the driver before you sign. Every bike also comes with 30 days' warranty and our 14-day money back.

Can I collect instead?

Yes. Collection from the showroom is free. Change your choice any time before your bike is ready by calling us.
