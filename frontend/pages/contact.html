All FAQs
Do I need to book a viewing?

It helps. Book a time and we will have the bike out and ready. You are welcome to drop in during opening hours too.

Can I test ride a bike?

Yes, by appointment. Bring your licence and your own kit. Call or use the form above to book.

I live a long way away. Can I still buy?

Yes. Watch the video, book a live walkaround, reserve online for £99 and we deliver anywhere in mainland UK.

Can I sell my bike to you without buying one?

Yes. Get a valuation on our sell your bike page and we will make you an offer.
