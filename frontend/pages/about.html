Our story

Riders selling bikes to riders

We started GearUp because buying a used bike should not feel like a gamble.

We are a small team, and we keep stock small on purpose. Around 24 bikes at a time means we know every one of them. We know where it came from, what we have done to it and how it rides.

Every bike goes through our own workshop before it goes on sale. It gets an HPI check, a 120-point inspection and any work it needs. If a bike does not meet our standard, we do not sell it.

You do not have to live nearby. We film a walkaround of every bike, take a £99 reservation online and deliver across mainland UK, with live GPS tracking on the day.

How we prep a bike

From trade-in to ready to ride

Every bike follows the same route through our workshop before you see it online.

  1. HPI and history checkFinance, theft and write-off checks, plus the service book and MOT history.
  2. 120-point inspectionEngine, brakes, chassis, electrics and tyres, all signed off by a technician.
  3. Service and fixAnything that fails gets put right. Fresh MOT where needed.
  4. Valet, photos and videoCleaned, photographed and filmed, marks and all. Then it goes online.
