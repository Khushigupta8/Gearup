This Insurance Information Notice (sometimes called an IDD notice) gives you important information about how we arrange motorbike insurance quotes. Please read it before you ask for a quote.

## 1. Who we are

GearUp Motorcycles is a trading name of [Company legal name], company number [Company number], of [Registered office address].

## 2. Our regulatory status

We are an Introducer Appointed Representative (IAR) of [Insurance partner legal name], which is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), firm reference number [Partner FRN].

As an Introducer Appointed Representative, we can only introduce you to [Insurance partner name] and pass on your details. We cannot give you advice or a recommendation about insurance, and we do not arrange or sell the insurance ourselves.

You can check this on the FCA Register at register.fca.org.uk or by calling the FCA on 0800 111 6768.

## 3. Whose products are offered

[Insurance partner name] is a price comparison service that searches a panel of UK motorbike insurers. It does not search the whole market. Quotes are shown from the panel of insurers it works with.

## 4. No advice

We and [Insurance partner name] do not give advice. You will need to decide for yourself whether a policy meets your needs. Please read the policy documents, including the key information document, before you buy.

## 5. How we are paid

The quote service is free for you to use. If you buy a policy after being introduced by us, we may receive a commission from [Insurance partner name]. This does not change the price you pay. You can ask us how much commission we receive.

## 6. Your information

When you ask for a quote, your details are passed to [Insurance partner name] so they can show you quotes. See our [privacy policy](/legal/privacy) and the partner's own privacy notice.

## 7. Complaints

If you are unhappy with our service in introducing you, contact us first using our [complaints procedure](/legal/complaints). Complaints about the quote service or a policy should go to [Insurance partner name] or the insurer.

If your complaint is not resolved, you may be able to refer it to the Financial Ombudsman Service free of charge: financial-ombudsman.org.uk or 0800 023 4567.

## 8. Financial Services Compensation Scheme

Insurers are covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS). You may be entitled to compensation if an insurer cannot meet its obligations. Find out more at fscs.org.uk or on 0800 678 1100.
