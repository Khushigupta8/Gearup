This policy explains how to cancel an order, return a motorbike or accessory, and get a refund. It sits alongside our [terms and conditions](/legal/terms) and does not affect your legal rights.

## 1. Cancelling a bike you bought at a distance

If you buy a bike without visiting us in person (for example, you reserve online or over the phone and we deliver it), the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 give you 14 days to cancel. The 14 days start on the day you receive the bike.

To cancel, tell us clearly within the 14 days by phone, email or in writing. You do not have to give a reason.

Whether you bought online or at the showroom, if the bike is not right for you, you can return it within 14 days of collection or delivery.

## 4. How refunds work

- We refund everything you paid for the bike, including any delivery charge, within 14 days of getting the bike back.
- We refund using the same payment method you used, unless we agree otherwise.
- If the bike was bought on finance, we tell the lender and the finance agreement is cancelled.
- If you part-exchanged a bike, we return it to you if we still have it. If we have sold it, we refund the agreed part-exchange value.
- We may reduce the refund if the bike has been damaged, or used more than needed to check it, while it was with you.

## 5. Accessories

- You can return unused items in their original packaging within 14 days of receiving them, for a full refund.
- Please contact us first and quote your order number. You pay the cost of posting the item back unless it is faulty or we sent the wrong item.
- For hygiene reasons, we cannot accept returns of [items such as helmets, gloves or under-garments] once worn, unless they are faulty.

## 6. Faulty goods

If a bike or accessory is faulty, contact us straight away. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, you may be entitled to a repair, a replacement or a refund. For used bikes, we take account of the age, mileage and price of the bike. See our [used motorcycle condition and warranty policy](/legal/vehicle-condition).
