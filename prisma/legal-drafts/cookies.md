Cookies are small files a website saves on your device. This page explains the ones our website uses.

## Cookies we use

Our website only uses cookies and similar storage that it needs to work. These do not need your consent, and they do not track you across other websites.

- **Sign-in cookie**: keeps you signed in to your GearUp account or our staff system. It is removed when you sign out or it expires.
- **Basket and saved preferences**: your accessories basket and some display choices are saved in your browser, so they are still there when you come back. They never leave your device unless you check out.
- **Security**: our hosting provider may use cookies to protect the website from attacks and spam.

## Payments

When you pay, you go to our payment provider, Stripe. Stripe uses its own cookies to process the payment securely and prevent fraud. See Stripe's cookie policy at stripe.com/cookies-policy.

## Embedded videos

Some bike pages may show walkaround videos from YouTube or Vimeo. When you play one, that service may set its own cookies.

## Analytics

We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If we add them in the future, we will ask for your permission first and update this page.

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the essential cookies, signing in and checkout will not work.
