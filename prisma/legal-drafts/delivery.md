This policy explains how you can get your bike or accessories: collection from our showroom, or delivery to your door anywhere in the UK.

## 1. Collecting your bike

Collection from our showroom is free. We call you to agree a date and time once your bike is ready and the balance has been paid.

Please bring your driving licence, proof of insurance from the collection date and, if you are riding away, your own riding kit.

## 2. Bike delivery

- We deliver nationwide using our own driver and van. We do not use third-party couriers for bikes.
- Delivery is priced per mile by road from our showroom at [rate] per mile, with a minimum charge of [amount]. We confirm the exact price before we book your delivery.
- For islands and remote areas, please call us before you reserve.
- The delivery charge is paid with the balance, before the bike leaves us.

## 3. Delivery day

- We agree the delivery date with you in advance and give you an arrival window.
- You, or someone you trust aged 18 or over, must be there to receive the bike.
- Please check the bike over with our driver before signing the handover checklist. We hand over the keys, the V5C logbook and all documents in person.
- If nobody is available when we arrive, we may charge for a second delivery attempt.

## 4. Inspection on arrival

If you notice any damage or a problem when the bike arrives, tell our driver before signing, and note it on the handover checklist. If you find something later, contact us within 48 hours with photos. This does not affect your legal rights or your [14-day money back guarantee](/legal/returns).

## 5. When the bike becomes yours

The bike becomes your responsibility once it has been handed over to you or collected. Make sure your insurance starts from that day.
