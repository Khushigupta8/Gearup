## 2. What we collect

Depending on how you use our website and services, we may collect:

- **Contact details**: your name, email address, mobile number and postcode or address.
- **Enquiry details**: what you ask us about, the bike you are interested in and your preferred contact times.
- **Your bike**: registration, mileage, condition, service history and photos, if you ask for a part-exchange or sell your bike to us.
- **Finance applications**: date of birth, address history, marital status, employment, income and outgoings, so lenders can assess your application.
- **Insurance quotes**: date of birth, licence type and where the bike is kept.
- **Your account**: login details (your password is stored encrypted), saved bikes and preferences.
- **Orders and payments**: what you reserve or buy, delivery details and payment status. Card payments are handled by our payment provider, Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
- **Workshop bookings**: your bike's details and the work you need.
- **Website use**: technical information such as your browser and device, kept in server logs for security.

## 3. How we use it and our lawful basis

- **To answer your enquiries and arrange viewings, reservations, sales, delivery and workshop work.** Lawful basis: to take steps at your request before a contract, and to perform our contract with you.
- **To process finance applications and arrange insurance quotes.** Lawful basis: to take steps at your request before a contract.
- **To keep sales, accounts and tax records.** Lawful basis: legal obligation.
- **To keep our website and systems secure and prevent fraud.** Lawful basis: our legitimate interests.
- **To tell you about new stock, offers and services.** Lawful basis: your consent. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in our emails or by contacting us.
- **To improve our service, for example by understanding which enquiries lead to sales.** Lawful basis: our legitimate interests.
