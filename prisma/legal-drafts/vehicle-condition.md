We want you to know exactly what you are buying. This policy explains how we prepare our used motorbikes, how we describe their condition, and what the warranty covers.

## 1. How we prepare every bike

Before a bike goes on sale, it has:

- an HPI check, so it is not recorded as stolen, scrapped, written off or with outstanding finance;
- our 120-point inspection of the engine, brakes, suspension, wheels and tyres, electrics, frame and bodywork, followed by a road test;
- an MOT where one is due;
- a full valet.

Any work we carry out is recorded in the bike's history.

## 2. How we describe condition

Our bikes are used, so they may show normal signs of age and use for their mileage. We photograph any marks, scratches or wear we find and show them in the "Detailed pictures" section of each bike page. Service receipts and invoices we hold are shown in the History tab.

If you would like to see anything more closely, ask us for extra photos, a walkaround video or a live video call before you buy.

## 3. Warranty included with every bike

- Every bike comes with a 30-day warranty as standard.
- Some bikes include longer cover free of charge: 3, 6, 12 or 24 months. This is shown on the bike's badge and listing.
- You can buy longer cover on most bikes when you reserve or buy.
- The warranty starts on the day you collect the bike or we deliver it.

Warranty cover is provided by [Warranty provider name]. Your warranty booklet sets out exactly what is covered, the claim limits and what you need to do to keep the cover valid.

## 4. What the warranty usually does not cover

- Parts that wear out with normal riding, such as tyres, brake pads and discs, chains and sprockets, clutch plates, bulbs and batteries.
- Damage from accidents, misuse, racing or track days, or modifications.
- Faults caused by missing a service. The bike must be serviced on time by a qualified garage, and you should keep the receipts.

## 5. If something goes wrong

1. Stop riding the bike if it is not safe.
2. Call or message us with your order number and a description of the fault.
3. We will tell you how to claim and, if needed, arrange for the bike to be inspected.

Please contact us before any repair is carried out, otherwise the warranty may not pay for it.

## 6. Your legal rights

The warranty is in addition to your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015. It does not replace them. If a bike was not of satisfactory quality, not as described or not fit for purpose when you bought it, you have legal rights to a repair, replacement or refund.
